# 🏎️ Automotive Engineering & Power Systems
### Kinematic Analysis | Mechanical Assembly | Precision CAD

This folder contains high-fidelity 3D models of internal combustion engine components. These projects demonstrate my ability to handle complex assemblies, manage multi-part constraints, and design for both mechanical simulation and physical prototyping.

---

## 📂 Project 1: V6 Internal Combustion Engine (`Engine_V6.f3d`)

A comprehensive 6-cylinder engine assembly developed in **Autodesk Fusion 360**. This project was a deep dive into the synchronization of moving parts within a high-performance engine block.



### 🛠 Technical Highlights:
* **Complex Kinematics:** Fully defined revolute and slider joints ensuring that the crankshaft rotation accurately drives the piston stroke across all six cylinders.
* **Assembly Management:** Utilized a "Bottom-Up" assembly approach, ensuring each component (Connecting Rods, Pistons, Wrist Pins, and Crankshaft) maintains proper tolerances and clearances.
* **Interference Checking:** Conducted motion studies to verify zero-interference between the rotating counterweights and the engine housing.
* **Design Detail:** Features accurate cylinder bank angles and realistic piston-head geometry.

## 📂 Project 2: Piston Engine Prototype (`Piston_Engine.stl`)

This project focuses on **Design for Additive Manufacturing (DfAM)**. It is a streamlined piston mechanism optimized for 3D printing and educational demonstrations.



### 🛠 Technical Highlights:
* **3D Print Optimization:** Exported as a high-resolution `.stl` file with geometry refined to minimize the need for support structures during the printing process.
* **Prototyping Focus:** Designed with increased tolerances to ensure smooth motion after physical assembly using FDM or SLA printing technologies.
* **Educational Value:** Designed to clearly showcase the conversion of reciprocating linear motion into rotational energy, making it an ideal model for mechanical demonstrations.

## 🔬 Applied Engineering Skills

* **Constraint Logic:** Advanced use of "As-Built" and "Joint" constraints in Fusion 360.
* **Motion Studies:** Simulating real-world engine timing and mechanical cycles.
* **Material Selection:** Designing with specific structural properties in mind for future FEA (Finite Element Analysis).
* **DfM (Design for Manufacturing):** Balancing aesthetic CAD modeling with the practical realities of 3D printing and machining.
