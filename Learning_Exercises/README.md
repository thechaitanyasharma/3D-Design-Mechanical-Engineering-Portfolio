# 📚 CAD Learning & Skill Development
### Foundational Principles | Parametric Modeling | Sketch Constraints

This folder serves as a record of my technical progression in **Autodesk Fusion 360**. These exercises were designed to push the boundaries of my modeling efficiency, focusing on creating robust, editable timelines and mastering complex geometric relationships.

---

## 📂 Featured Exercises

### 1. Exercise 02: Complex Sketching & Constraints
A deep dive into 2D sketch geometry and the power of parametric constraints.



* **Parametric Logic:** Focused on creating a "fully defined" sketch where all dimensions and relationships (Parallel, Tangent, Coincident) are locked, ensuring the model doesn't break when parameters are changed.
* **Feature Mastery:** Implementation of **Pattern** and **Mirror** tools to create symmetrical complexity without redundant sketching.
* **Workflow:** Practiced the "Big to Small" workflow—sketching the primary silhouette before adding intricate internal cutouts.

---

### 2. Exercise 03: 3D Feature Manipulation
An exercise focused on transforming 2D profiles into complex 3D volumes using advanced modeling features.



* **Feature Set:** Utilizing **Revolve**, **Sweep**, and **Loft** commands to create geometry that simple extrusions cannot achieve.
* **Fillet & Chamfer Strategy:** Strategic application of edge treatments at the end of the timeline to maintain model stability.
* **Boolean Operations:** Mastering the use of **Combine (Join/Cut/Intersect)** to build parts through the interaction of multiple bodies.

---

## 🔬 Core Competencies Developed

* **Timeline Management:** Keeping a clean, organized design history that allows for easy "Rollback" and editing.
* **Constraint Discipline:** Understanding why "Blue lines" (unconstrained) are the enemy of professional CAD work.
* **Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing (GD&T):** Learning how to translate 2D technical drawings with specific tolerances into accurate 3D representations.
* **Efficiency:** Developing a "First-Time-Right" mindset to reduce the number of features in the browser tree.

---

## 🚀 How to Explore

* **Fusion 360:** Open the `.f3d` files to view the sketches and features. I encourage you to drag the timeline marker back to the beginning to see exactly how these parts were built from scratch.
