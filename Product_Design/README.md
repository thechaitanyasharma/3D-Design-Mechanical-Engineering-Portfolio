# 🎨 Consumer Product Design & Ergonomics
### Surface Modeling | Industrial Design | Aesthetic Functionality

This folder showcases a variety of consumer product designs that bridge the gap between artistic vision and mechanical reality. These projects, developed in **Autodesk Fusion 360**, emphasize ergonomic user interfaces, clean topology, and design for modern manufacturing.

---

## 📂 Featured Projects

### 1. Computer Mouse Design
An exploration into ergonomic surface modeling, designed to fit the natural contours of the human hand to reduce strain during prolonged use.



* **Surface Continuity:** Focused on achieving **G2 curvature continuity** across the main body to ensure seamless light reflections and a premium feel.
* **Ergonomics:** Designed through iterative sculpting to optimize the "palm-grip" and "claw-grip" support.
* **Internal Layout:** Modeled with mounting points for standard optical sensors and tactile switches.

---

### 2. Electric Kettle
A project combining industrial design aesthetics with fluid dynamics and heat management considerations.



* **Form & Function:** Features a minimalist, organic silhouette with a handle designed for a balanced center of gravity during pouring.
* **Material Study:** Designed with distinct material properties in mind—brushed stainless steel for the body and heat-resistant polymer for the interface.
* **Detailing:** Includes a precision-modeled spout for a controlled, drip-free water flow.

---

### 3. 3D Printed Lampshade
A creative project optimized specifically for **Additive Manufacturing (3D Printing)**, focusing on light diffusion and complex geometry.



* **Generative Patterns:** Developed using complex geometric patterns that create unique shadow textures when illuminated.
* **Vase-Mode Optimization:** Specifically designed to be printed as a single continuous extrusion (Spiral Vase Mode), ensuring a lightweight yet structurally sound product.
* **Light Diffusion:** The thickness and "lattice-like" structures were calculated to provide optimal light scattering for a warm, ambient environment.

---

## 🔬 Design & Aesthetic Competencies

* **Organic Sculpting:** Using T-Splines and Sculpting environments to create smooth, complex shapes.
* **Surface Quality:** Ensuring high-quality surface finishes (Class-A surfacing) for consumer-facing products.
* **Human-Centered Design:** Applying anthropometric data to ensure products are comfortable and intuitive to use.
* **Design for Additive Manufacturing (DfAM):** Crafting geometries that are impossible to manufacture via traditional methods but perfect for 3D printing.
